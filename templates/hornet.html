Hornet (Silksong)

From The Silk Web, the comprehensive spoiler-free character database repository.

Hornet is the protagonist of Hollow Knight: Silksong. As the princess-protector of Hallownest, she finds herself captured and brought to the unfamiliar, haunted kingdom of Pharloom, a land ruled by silk and song.

Abilities & Weaving Combat

Unlike her combat behaviours observed in previous database encounters, Hornet utilizes advanced silk-weaving disciplines to traverse environmental hazards and dispatch hostiles within Pharloom:

  • Silkspear: Hornet throws the Needle forward, piercing enemies. The Silkspear deals 3× Needle Damage on hit.
  • Thread Storm: Creates a whirl of Silk centred on Hornet that strikes multiple times and destroys most weak projectiles.
  • Cross Stitch: Hornet raises the Needle in a defensive stance before counterattacking with a flurry of strikes.
