Hornet (Silksong)

From The Silk Web, the comprehensive spoiler-free character database repository.

Hornet is the protagonist of Hollow Knight: Silksong. As the princess-protector of Hallownest, she finds herself captured and brought to the unfamiliar, haunted kingdom of Pharloom, a land ruled by silk and song.

Abilities & Weaving Combat

Unlike her combat behaviors observed in previous database encounters, Hornet utilizes advanced silk-weaving disciplines to traverse environmental hazards and dispatch hostiles within Pharloom:

  • Silkspear: Hornet throws the Needle forward, piercing enemies. The Silkspear deals 3x Needle Damage on hit.
  • Thread Storm: Creates a whirl of Silk centered on Hornet that strikes 5 times, which can be extended to 11 times via repeated input of the SKILL/TOOL button. Thread Storm deals 0.75x Needle Damage in the first 4 hits, the 5th hit deals 0.25x Needle Damage, and the remaining hits deal damage equal to current Needle Level (minimum of 1). Thread storm will destroy most weak projectiles.
  • Cross Stitch: Hornet raises the Needle in a defensive stance, countering with a flurry of 4 attacks upon blocking a hit. Anything that is able to hurt Hornet can be blocked by Cross Stitch, including enemy contact damage. Cross Stitch deals 0.75x Needle Damage on hit, rounding up, resulting in a total of 3x Needle Damage if all hits connect.
