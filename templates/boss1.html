Lace

Lace is a recurring NPC and boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong. A deadly fencer wielding a gilded pin, she is a being woven entirely from the Silk of her mother. She battles Hornet twice within the Deep Docks and the Cradle to prevent her from reaching the peak of the Citadel. Lost Lace is her powerful void-possessed form.

Background

Lace is the daughter of Grand Mother Silk, spun entirely from threads of her Silk. The Mother created her to be a loyal child who would want the Mother to wake up from her slumber, unlike her other creations, the Weavers. Her body was spun pure yet frail, shaped to have the look and mind of a child. Phantom is her sibling, but they became frayed and were discarded, their Silk stretched thin and their shell stiffened.

In-game Events

Lace is a recurring antagonist to Hornet, and can be found throughout her journey in Pharloom. She can be encountered in every Act of the game.

Act 1

  • Lace conducting Silk Flies in the Deep Docks Lace can be first encountered in Deep Docks after acquiring Swift Step. She is conducting the movement of Silk Flies and talks about Hornet's fall from the cage she was captured in, potentially suggesting she was the one who was responsible for the fall.[15] She fights Hornet at the end of the conversation, stating she wants to save Hornet from the suffering she would have to face above.[16]

Behaviour and Tactics

In her first encounter and fight in the Deep Docks, Lace uses the following attacks and manoeuvres: Lunge: Lace dashes forwards across the platform with her pin extended, covering roughly 60% of it. Double Cut: Lace slashes twice, making large arcs over her head. Each slash moves her forwards a slight distance. Balestra: Lace launches herself at an angle into the air and slashes a full circle around her body with her pin at the height of the jump. She then lunges diagonally downwards, skidding a short distance upon landing. While at the peak of her jump, Lace can change the direction of her lunge depending on Hornet's position.
